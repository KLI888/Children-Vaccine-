## 3. Optional / Private Sector Vaccines (IAP Category)

These are categorized separately with clear labels so parents understand they are optional additions commonly discussed in private clinics:
1. **Typhoid Conjugate Vaccine (TCV):** Recommended at 6–9 months (single dose).
2. **Hepatitis A:** Inactivated (2 doses at 12 & 18 months) or Live attenuated (single dose at 12 months).
3. **Varicella (Chickenpox):** Dose 1 at 15 months, Dose 2 at 3–6 years.
4. **Influenza (Flu):** Annually starting from 6 months of age.
5. **Meningococcal & Cholera:** For high-risk children or outbreak areas.

## 4. Guidance for Delayed or Missed Doses

1. **No Need to Restart:** According to WHO and MoHFW catch-up rules, if a child is delayed in receiving a dose, do **not** restart the schedule from Dose 1. Administer the delayed dose and maintain the minimum interval before the next dose.
2. **Minimum Intervals:**
   - Between Pentavalent/DPT doses: at least 4 weeks.
   - Between live injectable vaccines (e.g., BCG, MR) if not given on the same day: at least 4 weeks.
3. **Upper Age Limits:**
   - BCG can be administered up to 1 year of age.
   - Pentavalent is given up to 1 year; beyond 1 year, DPT is used up to 7 years.
   - Rotavirus is typically not initiated after 8 months.
4. **Professional Clinical Review:** The app will **never** automatically recalculate subsequent doses without pediatric consultation. If a dose is overdue, the app flags the delay, displays the elapsed duration, and advises immediate consultation with an authorized primary health centre (PHC) or pediatrician.

## 5. Technical Decisions & Architectural Summary

- **Three-Stage Reminder System:** 
  - Stage 1: Exactly 7 calendar days before the due date.
  - Stage 2: Exactly 3 calendar days before the due date.
  - Stage 3: Day of vaccination (0 days).
  - Time zone-aware calculations based on Asia/Kolkata (`UTC+05:30`).
  - Idempotency key pattern: `{child_id}:{vaccine_id}:{dose_number}:{stage}:{due_date_iso}`.
  - Duplication protection prevents multiple deliveries across cron loops.
- **Data Isolation:** User-level Row Level Security (RLS) on all patient tables (`profiles`, `children`, `child_vaccinations`, `reminders`, `growth_records`, `symptom_records`).
- **Bilingual Interface:** Full English and Marathi translations for non-technical parents and ASHA/Anganwadi workers.
